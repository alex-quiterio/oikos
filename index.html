Line weights are relative guesses for comparing flows, not measured fluxes. The scenario model is a toy: it multiplies each flow by the supply of its source and marks an organism short when a type of input it receives falls, using only documented and likely flows. It shows direction and exposure, never biomass. Real food webs have alternatives that are missing here, and debated and unknown flows stay out of the arithmetic.
